Errata
======

After the publication of the paper, the following errors were identified in the
Supporting Information of the Binding Curve Viewer manuscript. The corrected
versions are reflected in :doc:`appendix1` of this documentation.

----

**(1)** In the Supporting Information, equation Appendix 1.4 :eq:`ap_1.4` contained a sign error
in the coefficient of :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq}`. The incorrect equation
was:

.. math::

    {(\left[\mathrm{PL}\right]_\mathrm{eq})}^2-\left(\left[\mathrm{P}\right]_0+\left[\mathrm{L}\right]_0-K_\mathrm{d}\right)\left[\mathrm{PL}\right]_\mathrm{eq}+\left[\mathrm{P}\right]_0\left[\mathrm{L}\right]_0=0

The correct equation is:

.. math::

    {(\left[\mathrm{PL}\right]_\mathrm{eq})}^2-\left(\left[\mathrm{P}\right]_0+\left[\mathrm{L}\right]_0+K_\mathrm{d}\right)\left[\mathrm{PL}\right]_\mathrm{eq}+\left[\mathrm{P}\right]_0\left[\mathrm{L}\right]_0=0

The minus sign before :math:`K_\mathrm{d}` has been corrected to a plus sign.

----

**(2)** In the Supporting Information, the inequality describing the relationship
between :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}` and :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`
was stated incorrectly. The incorrect statement was:

    From the expressions of :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}` and
    :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`, we know that
    :math:`0>[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}>[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`.

The correct statement is:

    From the expressions of :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}` and
    :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`, we know that
    :math:`0<[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}<[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`.

The direction of the inequalities has been corrected: both
:math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}` and :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq2}`
are positive quantities, and :math:`[\mathrm{PL}]_\mathrm{eq1}` is the
physically meaningful (smaller) root of equation :eq:`ap_1.4`.
